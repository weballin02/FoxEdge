# FoxEdge

FoxEdge builds evidence-led historical sports research and decision-support tools.

This repository contains development work for FoxEdge research systems. Bettor-facing claims must be tied to data, weak spots are filtered, and uncertainty remains visible.

## MLB Historical Research Library

The FoxEdge MLB Historical Research Library organizes 13,661 historical MLB games and 90 documented research situations into:

- a full research playbook (PDF)
- a searchable 90-row research database (Excel)
- a quick-reference guide
- a start and usage guide
- evidence grades, supplied holdout fields, cautions, and rejected findings

[View the FoxEdge MLB Historical Research Library](https://foxedge.gumroad.com/l/mlb-research?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repository&utm_campaign=mlb-research-library&utm_content=readme)
